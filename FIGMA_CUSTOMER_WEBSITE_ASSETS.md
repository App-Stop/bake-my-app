### Delivery demo behavior

The cart opens as a drawer. Checkout supports delivery or pickup and cash payment, preserves contact/address/instructions on cancellation, and calculates all totals in cents. `COZY20` applies a 20% demo discount before VAT. Once the five-second cancellation window completes, tracking advances while visible: preparing (8 seconds), delivery route (12 seconds), rider at address (10 seconds), then delivered. Clicking the status/map also advances the demo. Rider tips are separate cash tips, retained separately from the order total. Ratings and comments are retained in the local demo session. This demo sends no payments or orders to a server.

Use **Pickup instead** on delivery checkout to select pickup, or **Deliver Instead** to return. Contact details are shared; delivery and pickup locations and instructions are stored separately. Pickup has no delivery fee, priority option, leave-at-door switch, or rider tip. The payment copy changes to cash at the counter. The cancellation dialog shows the selected pickup location.

After submission, pickup tracking advances from preparing (8 seconds) to ready (10 seconds), then order complete. Clicking a status card also advances the demo. Ready uses the shop map and the exact markers and collection illustration shown in Figma. The completion dialog shows the pickup points/QR panel and saves the rating and comment in the local demo session. Submitted orders retain their own fulfillment mode if checkout preferences change later.

The website cart uses the new 540 × 1058 design with 20px rounded corners on all sides, the original 250px outer shadow, inset placement, a 50px panel-close control, 97px product rows for products with quantity offers, and the original footer padding.
